Device configuration for POCO F7 / Redmi Turbo 4 Pro
=========================================

The POCO F7 / Redmi Turbo 4 Pro (codename: onyx) is a high-performance flagship-tier device featuring the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset and a stunning 6.85-inch AMOLED display.

## Device specifications

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
SoC     | Qualcomm Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 (sm8735)
CPU     | Octa-core (1x3.21 GHz & 2x2.80 GHz & 3x3.01 GHz & 2x2.02 GHz)
GPU     | Adreno 825
Memory  | 12GB/16GB RAM (LPDDR5X), 256GB/512GB/1TB UFS 4.0
Shipped Android Version | Android 15 (API 35), HyperOS
Battery | 7500 mAh (Typical), non-removable
Dimensions | ~160.5 x 74.4 x 7.8 mm
Display | AMOLED, 120Hz, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, 6.85 inches, 1280 x 2772 pixels (~447 ppi)
Rear Camera 1 | 50.3 MP, f/1.5, (wide), PDAF, OIS, 8K Video
Rear Camera 2 | 8 MP, (ultrawide)
Front Camera | 20.2 MP, f/2.2, (wide)
